Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Overview

DuoSave RWF is a private personal finance and shared savings tracker designed for couples. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring your financial information remains confidential.

2. Information We Collect

The application only stores the savings targets, monthly deposits, loans, repayments, and partner names that you explicitly input into the app.

3. How Your Data is Used

All financial records are used solely within your private account instance to calculate balances, loan repayments, and monthly savings goals. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with advertisers or third parties.

4. Data Security & Storage

Your data is saved securely in your designated private database. You also have the right and ability to export, backup, or delete your entire history at any time directly through the app settings.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding this privacy policy, you can contact the app maintainer directly through your private deployment.
